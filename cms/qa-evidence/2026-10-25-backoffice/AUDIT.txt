REQUIRED BACK-OFFICE CAPABILITIES / CURRENT STATUS
1. Identity and access — doctor/admin UI exists; server-side authentication, role enforcement, sessions and least-privilege API access remain integration work. Browser role flags are not authorization.
2. Clinical intake and consent — submitted encounter, questionnaire, attachments and consent fields rendered; durable versioned consent records and secure file storage required on server.
3. Queue, appointments and availability — local cross-tab booking, conflicts, reassignment, callback, cancellation, no-show and follow-up flows tested. Server-side atomic reservation, timezone policy, reminders and cross-device sync required.
4. Clinical consultation — local encounter transitions, chat, private notes, prescription draft and confirmed patient plan tested. Real video/chat transport, clinician identity and clinical review remain external integrations.
5. History and documents — versioned local documents/revocation/download tested. Signed prescriptions, access-controlled durable records and legal retention remain backend responsibilities; unsigned preview is retained as such.
6. Orders and pharmacy — fixture delivery states and patient/doctor rendering tested. Real pharmacy stock, fulfilment callbacks, delivery provider events, retries and reconciliation require APIs. Golden fixture tests do not certify live orders.
7. Finance — pricing/promotion configuration currently saves browser-only settings; it does NOT drive patient checkout. Payment capture/refunds, invoices, reconciliation and server-authoritative prices are outstanding integration work. Do not label browser save as a production price change.
8. CMS — approved initial collection, draft/review/publish snapshots and local recipient refresh tested. Cross-device publication, editor/reviewer separation and immutable change history require authenticated content APIs.
9. Operations — queue counts and local audit view exist. Production alerting, support ownership, operational SLA queues, event retries and logs must be built against real event sources.
10. Reviews — local ratings flow tested. Programme marketing aggregates/sample comments are not verified customer reviews and must not be promoted to real data without an approved review source.

LIMITS
No real patient data, clinical prescription, payment, refund, or external message was sent in this audit. Local storage workflows synchronize within one origin/browser context only. Cross-device/production persistence and service permissions were not verified. Do not call the whole back office production-ready from these frontend checks.
