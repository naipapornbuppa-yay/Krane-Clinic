# Weight medicine die-cut image sources

These transparent PNGs are presentation assets for the landing-page medicine carousel. Product choice and suitability remain subject to a physician's assessment.

- `mounjaro-kwikpen-diecut-v1.png` — background-extracted and recomposed from Eli Lilly's official Mounjaro KwikPen family image. The clearer six-pen reference replaces the previous low-detail image:
  - https://www.lilly.com/en-ca/medicines/medicine-faqs/mounjaro-kwikpen
  - https://delivery-p137454-e1438138.adobeaemcloud.com/adobe/assets/urn%3Aaaid%3Aaem%3A124bf903-acab-40cd-a68d-dadb27d4695a/as/kwikpen.avif?assetname=kwikpen.png
- `ozempic-pens-diecut-v1.png` — official Novo Nordisk transparent Ozempic pen-family image, copied without a background:
  - https://s7d9.scene7.com/is/image/novonordisk/2-1-three-ozempic-pens-1%3A16by9?dpr=off&fmt=png-alpha
- `ozempic-pens-logo-redacted-v2.png` — presentation-safe derivative of the official Ozempic image above. The final `C` has been removed from each of the three large product wordmarks while the rest of the pen artwork remains intact; the chroma background was then removed locally.
- `wegovy-flex-touch-diecut-v1.png` — background-extracted from Novo Nordisk's official Wegovy pen-family image:
  - https://www.wegovy.com/content/dam/dct/wegovy/en/web/obesity/starting-wegovy-pen/Wegovy_pens_vertical.png
- `rybelsus-bottles-diecut-v1.png` — background and surrounding dosage callouts removed from Novo Nordisk's official Rybelsus bottle-family image:
  - https://www.rybelsus.com/content/dam/diabetes/niad/brand/rybelsus/images/icons/pill-bottles-desktop.png

The Mounjaro, Wegovy, and Rybelsus assets were isolated/recomposed with OpenAI image generation against a flat chroma background, then converted locally to transparent PNGs. They require the usual product, regulatory, and brand-usage approval before production use.
